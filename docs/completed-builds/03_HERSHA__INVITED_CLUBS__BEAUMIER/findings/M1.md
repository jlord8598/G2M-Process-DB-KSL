   as instructed. `Load` carries actuals; `Input` and `Central Load` carry

   - `EBITDA_SUMMARY` (28,043 rows) is a computed EBITDA stated by the source.
   - `TotalDOMEnter` ties to its daily rows in only 76% of groups, so it is
     borderline (Q-01).
3. **HERSHA's `GL_STAT` holds counts and hours in its amount column** (Q-02).
   The table and the `ST` prefix say statistic; the column says amount.
4. **No lane's numbering gives a usable §5 digit throughout.**
   - HERSHA's 6 and 7 ranges mix sections.
   - INVITED_CLUBS has 461 named members with no digit, and digit 5 is mostly
     revenue.
   - BEAUMIER has no account number at all.
   - Each lane therefore needs a stated-source map (Q-03, Q-05, Q-06).
5. **Two more lanes with sign conventions that `VALUE` will not net** (Q-04),
   the same issue as build 02's Q-22.
