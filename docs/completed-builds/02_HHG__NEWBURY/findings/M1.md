## Pacing and the two named tables

- **`OTB_1DAY_PICKUP` and `OTB_3DAY_PICKUP` are pacing, and it is derivable.**
  `REPORT_DATE` is an as-of distinct from `STAY_DATE` (18 and 10 report dates,
  each a September window in 2025 and 2026; stay dates to 2028-03-30).
  `DATA_TYPE` labels each row `ACTUALS` or an `OTB±n` position.
  `MARKET_SEGMENT` codes are not GL accounts. The two tables share 6 report
  dates and 16,908 identical rows; both are kept (Q-16).
- **`OPERATING_STATS_FCST` is also snapshot-shaped**: six report dates each
  repeating the same 365 stay dates. Proposed pacing (Q-12).
- **`OPERATING_STATS_SHOPS` is competitor rate shops** of eight comp-set names
  (one literally `~`), with an as-of `REPORTED_DATE`. Not the property's own
  money. Proposed pacing stream, own shape (Q-13).
- **`ROUNDS_DETAIL` is rounds played per club per day**, a statistic; proposed
  a statistics fact (Q-07). It runs to 2026-12-31 with zeros after the load.

## Most important findings

1. **Neither lane has a balance sheet.** Both charts run from section 4 to 9.
2. **HHG department 999 is probably statistics** (all whole numbers, exactly
   the digit-9 accounts), and nothing in the source says so (Q-01).
3. **HHG has no account descriptions at all** (Q-02), and its three fact
   tables identify a club three different ways with no complete map (Q-03).
4. **NEWBURY's `VALUE_TYPE` is a stated `RECORD_TYPE`**, and 11 keys need it
   to keep an amount row and a statistic row apart.
5. **HHG uses two sign conventions in one lane**: TR12 books expense positive,
   Anaplan negative. `SOURCE_AMOUNT` carries both as booked.
