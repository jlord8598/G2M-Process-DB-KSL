## Most important findings

1. **HHG Anaplan `VALUE` is not a P&L figure.**
   - Anaplan books expenses negative and the build signs by `NORMAL_BALANCE`,
     so `VALUE = SOURCE_AMOUNT x VALUE_SIGN` turns every expense positive.
   - The HHG forecast `VALUE` total is 1,428,944,200.12 against a
     `SOURCE_AMOUNT` of 325,117,641.88: revenue plus the absolute expense.
   - It also counts the `+All Regions` subtotal: 360 rows totalling
     162,558,820.94, equal to the detail.
   - `SOURCE_AMOUNT` is correct as booked. The fix for `VALUE` is a sign
     decision in the master (or a lane rule), not a repair here. See
     `classification-coverage.md`.
2. **NEWBURY BGT/PYACT room revenue is in `UNKNOWN`** (3,160 rows,
   249,229,415.81) pending Q-19.
3. **NEWBURY D&A and income tax sit in `Other Income / (Expense)`**: 11 keys
