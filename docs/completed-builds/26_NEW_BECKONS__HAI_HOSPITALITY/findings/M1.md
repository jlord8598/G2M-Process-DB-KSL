## 2. Subtotals, totals, profit lines
None in any table. BECKONS' "... - Total" and "Tierra Hotels Corporate EBITDA" are iLevel mapping buckets holding detail accounts. HAI's OVERHEAD is an allocation netting to zero within the lane. Nothing to exclude, and IS_SUBTOTAL FALSE by structure. The lesson of builds 24 / 25 does not bite here, but HAI's split periods are a grain trap: summing by PERIOD_END is correct, summing by CALENDAR_DATE splits periods across months.
