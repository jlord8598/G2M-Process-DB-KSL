## 2. DAILY vs MONTHLY Actuals
- Daily summed to the month equals monthly in 99.9% of shared cells in every section. For accounts in both tables, daily is a duplicate.
- Daily-only accounts: KSL 2,141 cells, PEREGRINE 83. None carry revenue or expense amounts; the KSL daily-only cells with amounts are digit 0 / 1 balance-sheet cells (18.8M).
- The "~12% / 77 site-months" figure is an artefact: daily PERIOD_END is the business date, not month-end.
- Daily-only site-months: KSL CAPICOP, 6 months of 2026; PEREGRINE CABRDOP and CAYRDOP, 24 months of 2024.

## 4. How build 13's rules let this through
- Build 01 measured the overlap (Q-09: "ledger duplicates daily; daily equals monthly on about 99.8%"). The owner chose guidance, not precedence: `reconciliation.md` tells consumers to filter on SOURCE_TABLE and never sum across. D-25 keeps everything.
- D-48 came later (build 10) and was never applied to these lanes. Builds 04, 05 and 13 rebuilt them for other reasons (chart, departments, subtotals) and repeated the guidance. Build 13's scope was calculated-line subtotals; it never re-tested the three-table overlap.
- Transformation sets CATEGORY ACTUAL by SOURCE_TABLE for all three, so they all reach SEM_AI_INCOME_STATEMENT_ACTUAL, and check 2(m) has no rule to test.
- D-50 lists neither lane, so the DEBIT-booked ledger counts as PRESENTED. VALUE = SOURCE_AMOUNT x VALUE_SIGN keeps its revenue negative, and it cancels monthly. A sum of VALUE over the actual table nets toward daily revenue in ledger site-months and doubles revenue elsewhere.
