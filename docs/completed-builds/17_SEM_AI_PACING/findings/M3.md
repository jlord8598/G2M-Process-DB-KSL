  - Q-03: NEWBURY rate shops excluded.

- **Two WHERE clauses, for your review:**
  - NEWBURY `source_table <> CLN_NEWBURY_BOSTON_OPS_RATE_SHOPS` (Q-03).
  - After the unpivot, a cell is kept if its (table, column) is declared, or its table is not in the declared list, or its value is not null. This drops only the all-null cells of skipped columns. A column that starts carrying values, or a new table, comes through and then breaks the row identity, so it cannot be lost silently. No transformation row is excluded.
