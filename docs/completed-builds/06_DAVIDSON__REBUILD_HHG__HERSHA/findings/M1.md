### `HOTEL_MONTHLY_REPORT`

- **Hotels.** 16 `HOTEL_CODE`s, never null. Some codes carry two names, for
  example `PMIGRA` is `The Davenport Grand` and `The Davenport Grand,
  Autograph Collection`. `RINGMIBIKE` (Mackinac Island Bike Shop) is in MR
  only.
- **`LINE_CLASS`.**
  - `Item`: 156,588 rows.
  - `ADR`: 180 rows. `Rooms`: 180 rows.
  - `ADR` and `Rooms` rows have a null `ACCOUNT` and hold an average rate
    (0-702.73) and a room count (0-15,090). `LINE_TYPE` is always `By
    Period`.
- **`ACCOUNT` on `Item` rows.**
  - 3,138 distinct strings, never blank and never padded.
  - 6 segments, `DEPT-NATURAL-XXXXX-XXXXX-xxxxx-xxxxx`, for example
    `PADMI-5000-XXXXX-XXXXX-MGTWG-ACMAN`.
  - Segment 1 is a department code (59 values, `PADMI` ... `PWATE`, plus
    `XXXXX` on 36 rows). It never equals the hotel code.
  - Segment 2 is a 4-digit natural account.
- **Malformed accounts.** 156 strings on 6,600 rows are one segment with an
  `x` prefix (`x402052000`, `xPMISC`, `xPSALE`). The dictionary decomposes
  none of them.
- **Natural-account leading digit** (6-segment `Item` rows; actual sum /
  rows):

| Digit | Naturals | Rows | Actual sum |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 21 (4101-4900) | 20,076 | 361,344,802 |
| 5 | 16 (5000-5990) | 89,844 | 227,486,870 |

| 7 | 6 (7000-7600) | 3,072 | 152,425,514 |
| 8 | 1 (8000) | 384 | 0 |
| 9 | 27 (9000-9990) | 36,480 | 6,178,080 |

- **The 9xxx naturals look like statistics, but the source does not say
  so.**
  - 9000 on `PROOM` holds whole numbers to 22,196; 9001 sits on the
    restaurants.
  - 9300 on `PENER` sums 68,090,316, and 9990 on `PSTAT` sums -71,858,974.
  - 9102 spans 51 departments and holds non-integer values on 10,163 of its
    24,996 rows.
  - See Q-07.
- **No descriptions.** The dictionary describes only `ADR` and `Rooms`. No
  `Item` string has an account or department description.
- **Periods.** Month-end `PERIOD_DATE`, 2026-01-31 to 2026-12-31.
- **Measures.**
  - Actual is null on 39,605 rows and forecast on 95,041. Never both.
  - Each measure's file path and row number are null exactly where its

- **Uniqueness.** Unique on hotel + class + account + period (0
  duplicates), and on the four file and row pointers + period.
- **Snapshots.** One per line: actual report month `2026-09-01` or
  `2026-07-01`, forecast `2026-09-01` or `2026-07-01`. No line appears at
  two as-of dates, so this is not pacing.

### `FULL_YEAR_PL`

- **No account code.** The line is identified by `REPORT_SHEET`, `SECTION`
  and `LINE_ITEM`:
  - 4,848 distinct line items;
  - 71 sections: 50 on `ALL_DEPARTMENT`, 21 on `SUMMARY`, none on both;
  - no blank and no padded value.
- **`ALL_DEPARTMENT`** (230,112 rows): sections are departments (`ROOMS`,
  `SPA`, `RESTAURANT 1` ... `UTILITIES`, `NON-OPERATING INCOME & EXPENSE`).
  Line items mix revenue, payroll, expense and `TOTAL` lines.
- **`SUMMARY`** (7,044 rows), in five section groups:

| Section | Lines |
|---|---|
| hotel heading (`PINAMC - Amara Cay Resort`, `[Islamorada]` ...) | `ADR`, `Available Rooms`, `Comp Rooms`, `Occupancy`, `Rooms RevPar`, `Rooms Sold`, `Total RevPar` |
| `Operating Revenues` | `Food and Beverage`, `Miscellaneous Income`, `Other Operated Departments`, `Rooms`, `Total Operating Revenue` |
| `Departmental Expenses` | `Departmental Profit`, `Food and Beverage`, `Other Operated Departments`, `Rooms`, `Total Departmental Expenses` |
| `Undistributed Operating Expenses` | `Administrative and General`, `Gross Operating Profit`, `House Profit`, `Information and Telecommunications Systems`, `Management Fees`, `Property Operations and Maintenance`, `Sales and Marketing`, `Total Undistributed Expenses`, `Utilities` |
| `Non-Operating Income and Expenses` | `EBITDA`, `EBITDA LESS REPLACEMENT RESERVE`, `FF&E Reserve Contribution`, `Insurance`, `Other`, `Other Income`, `Property and Other Taxes`, `Rent`, `Total Non-Operating` |

- **Roll-ups.**
  - `IS_ROLLUP` is true on 40,824 rows: `ALL_DEPARTMENT` 39,984 and
    `SUMMARY` 840.
  - Every one has a null `HOTEL_CODE` and `HOTEL_NAME` `Islamorada` or
    `Davenport Collection`.
  - No non-roll-up row has a null hotel code.
- **Hotels.** 15 codes, all present in MR.
- **Statistic lines.** The seven hotel-heading lines on `SUMMARY` are the
  only exact `ADR` / `Occupancy` / `Rooms Sold` / `RevPar` lines anywhere in
  the PL.
- **`TOTAL` lines.**
  - 57,420 rows on 1,209 line items begin with the word `Total`
    (case-insensitive); 47,172 of those rows are on non-roll-up
    `ALL_DEPARTMENT`.
  - Repeated names are disambiguated by the source itself, for example
    `TOTAL HOUSEKEEPING MANAGEMENT LABOR | #2`.
- **Repeated lines are distinct source lines.**
  - 12,684 rows repeat hotel + sheet + section + line + period.
  - Adding the four file and row pointers makes every row unique (0
    duplicates).
  - The pointers alone are not unique: 6,840 rows share all four pointers
    and the period with another row on a different sheet, section or
    line. One spreadsheet row feeds several report lines.
  - Kept as they are (staging is lossless).
- **Periods.** Month-end, 2026-01-31 to 2026-12-31.
- **Measures.**
  - Actual is null on 59,297 rows and forecast on 176,153. Never both.
  - Pointers are null exactly where their amount is null.
- **Snapshots.** Actual as-of `2026-10-01` or `2026-09-01`; forecast as-of
  `2026-09-01` or `2026-10-01`. Not pacing, for the same reason as MR.

### `ACCOUNTS`

- **Rows by source.** `HOTEL_MONTHLY_REPORT` 13,079 rows (3,140 strings, 16
  entities); `FULL_YEAR_PL` 19,763 rows (4,848 strings, 17 entities).
- **PL entities.** The PL entities are the 15 hotel codes plus `Islamorada`
  and `Davenport Collection`.
- **Not unique** on entity + `FULL_ACCOUNT_STRING` + `SOURCE_TABLE`.
  - There are 2,043 extra rows in 655 groups.
  - Within a group only `DEPARTMENT_DESCRIPTION` (up to 22 values) and
    `FIRST_SEEN_DATE` (up to 2) differ.
  - `ACCOUNT_DESCRIPTION` has one value per string.
- **MR strings.**
  - `DEPARTMENT_CODE` = segment 1, `ACCOUNT_CODE` = segment 2,
    `SUBACCOUNT_CODE` = segments 3-6, on all 12,487 decomposed rows.
  - The 592 undecomposed rows are the `x...` strings and `ADR` / `Rooms`.
- **PL strings.**
  - `FULL_ACCOUNT_STRING` = `LINE_ITEM`, `ACCOUNT_DESCRIPTION` =
    `LINE_ITEM`.
  - `DEPARTMENT_DESCRIPTION` = `SECTION` on 212,640 of 237,156 PL rows. The
    rest are lines that sit in more than one section (123 line items).
  - No codes.
- **Coverage.** Every fact line is in the dictionary: MR `Item` 156,588 of
  156,588 by string; PL 237,156 of 237,156 by string and entity. The
  dictionary is derived from the facts. It adds no code or section the
  facts lack.
- **`PARENT_CODE`** is always null and **`ALIAS`** always blank.

### Overlap between the facts

The same 15 hotels and 12 months appear in both facts. MR is GL account
detail and PL is the management P&L, so a consumer summing both counts a
hotel's money twice. `SOURCE_TABLE` separates them (Q-10).

### Roll-up verification

- **Verified: `SUMMARY` lines are totals of `ALL_DEPARTMENT`.**
  - `SUMMARY / Operating Revenues / Rooms` equals `ALL_DEPARTMENT / ROOMS /
    TOTAL ROOMS REVENUE` on 204 of 204 hotel-periods, exactly, actual and
    forecast (query `01c7edf9-020a-3bc3-0003-4ff21bc6ed5a`).
  - This is one line pair, a sample, not the whole sheet.
- **Not verifiable: `IS_ROLLUP` rows.**
  - The source does not say which hotels make up `Islamorada` or
    `Davenport Collection`.
  - On `SUMMARY / Rooms` for 2026-03-31, `Islamorada` (4,806,800) and
    `Davenport Collection` (6,223,077) match neither the `PIN*` nor the
    `PMI*` hotels' sum (query `01c7edf9-020a-3e71-0003-4ff21bc719b6`).
  - A code-prefix grouping matched 26% and 46% of lines (query
    `01c7edf6-020a-4652-0003-4ff21bc757b6`). That grouping was my guess,
    so the comparison is inconclusive.
  - The rule rests on the source's own flag (structure, stated).
- **`TOTAL` lines:** structure only. Without codes there is no detail to
  sum against.
