## Most important findings

1. **Record type is stated per account, not per row**, by chart
   `ACCOUNT_KIND`.
   - Paired account and stat-account rows are `STAT` in the chart but carry
     real P&L money.
   - On KSL, 5 account numbers occur in both shapes (23,843 rows), so
     `RECORD_TYPE` must be in the key.
   - Q-01 (blocks M3).
2. **Most daily rows have no account.** KSL 1,642,750 of 2,014,289 and
   PEREGRINE 234,814 of 323,986. Under D-06 they collapse to `~BLANK~` and
   route to `UNKNOWN`. Q-02 (blocks M3).
3. **Department is not on any fact row.** It is derivable from segment 1 of
   the account code. Q-03 (blocks M3).
4. **Daily and Monthly Actuals are the same actuals at two grains.** The
   ledger is a third, partial view of revenue and balance sheet. All are kept.
5. **`AS_OF_DATE` on daily is an extract timestamp**, one per site-day. The
   pace tables have a real as-of date but hold only a trailing window.
