**How each semantic model is built.**
- **Lane blocks.** It is a `UNION ALL` of two self-contained blocks between
  `-- BEGIN LANE <LANE>` and `-- END LANE <LANE>` (D-36). There are no shared
  CTEs, and columns are listed by name in section 4 order and cast to type.
- **The income statement split.** The three income statement models split
  `XFRM_{LANE}_INCOME_STATEMENT` on `CATEGORY`. It is a three-way partition:
  2(g) holds, and the counts add up below.
- **What the models do not do.** They read nothing but `TRANSFORMATION`, and
  aggregate nothing.
