## Q-03 correction (owner, stop 2)

**Rule:** where the COA gives a natural account one type at every department
it lists, that type also applies at the account's unlisted departments. Where
the account's types are mixed, unlisted departments keep today's seed.
Listed departments always take their own COA row.

**Effect** (query `01c82ae9-030a-7ce9-0003-4ff21c671e8a`): **2 keys** sit at
an unlisted department of a single-type account (Operating Expense, today
Expense / Payroll). Under the rule they move to Operating Expenses.

**Mixed-type accounts (unlisted departments keep today's seed):**

| Natural account | Description | COA types | Departments listed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 49950 | Misc Income | Other Income (950) / Revenue (901, 903, 904, 905, 906, 907, 908) | 901, 903-908, 950 |
| 51990 | Delivery/Fuel Surcharge (dept 903 row) / Rebates (dept 950 row) | Cost of Sales / Other Income | 903, 950 |
| 52100 | Inventory Adj Hard Goods | Cost of Sales / Operating Expense | 901, 906 |

**The owner's intent that "the 136 49xxx keys follow the 207" is not
delivered by this rule.** Those 136 are 49950 at listed departments typed
Revenue, so they stay Revenue. Making them follow needs an explicit
override: 49950 -> OI/E at every department. That is Q-09.
