## Q-10: the approved HERSHA routing conflicts with CLAUDE.md (stopped before M5)

**What Q-05 and Q-06 approve.** These rows go to UNKNOWN as
`DUPLICATE_DETAIL`:
- GL_ACTUAL ACTUAL rows at the 12 TB hotels;
- TB_ACTUAL FORECAST rows.

Their accounts are classified (H1 Revenue, Expense, ...), and their category
resolves.

**Why the classifier cannot do it.** TB and GL rows share the same five-part
keys. The split is by source table and category, row by row, so it has to
happen in `SPROC_XFRM_HERSHA`.

**What CLAUDE.md says:**
- **section 3:** `XFRM_{LANE}_UNKNOWN` holds "H1 = Unclassified, or the
  category does not resolve". Only PACING routes on source.
- **section 4:** a specific reason "replaces `UNCLASSIFIED_ACCOUNT` only
  (category resolved, `H1` unclassified)".
- **check 2(e):** "no row in a stream whose `H1` does not route there". It
  would fail on every such row.

**I proposed this routing at stop 1 without raising the conflict.**

**Proposed CLAUDE.md change (the owner's to make; not edited):**
- **section 3, the UNKNOWN stream line:** "H1 = Unclassified, or the category
  does not resolve, or the row is a measured duplicate of another source
  table of the lane (`DUPLICATE_DETAIL`, owner-approved per lane)".
- **section 4, after the reason list:** "`DUPLICATE_DETAIL` may also apply to
  a row whose account is classified, where the owner approves a row-level
  rule by source table and category that names the table it duplicates. Such
  a row keeps its H1-H5."
- **check 2(e):** "... except UNKNOWN rows with `UNKNOWN_REASON =
  DUPLICATE_DETAIL`".

**Alternatives:**
- (b) TB replaces GL. That loses hotels 118, 149 and 230 and GL's forecast.
- (c) Both as they are. That double counts.
